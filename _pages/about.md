<h2 id="about">Personal Information</h2>

**Affiliation:** School of Data Science and Statistics, Anhui University

**Position:** Lecturer

**Degree:** Ph.D.

**Email:** [zjsong@ahu.edu.cn](mailto:zjsong@ahu.edu.cn)

**Biography:** Zijie Song is currently a Lecturer at the School of Data Science and Statistics, Anhui University. He received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Hefei University of Technology in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Richang Hong. His research interests include multimedia technology, cross-modal understanding, vision-language learning, and their applications.

He has participated in several research projects supported by the National Key Research and Development Program of China and the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He has published more than ten papers in international journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, NeurIPS, and IJCAI. He also serves as a reviewer for international journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications, and ICME. He is a member of the Multimedia Technical Committee of the China Society of Image and Graphics.
